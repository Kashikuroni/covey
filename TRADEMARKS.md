# Covey Trademark Policy

This policy covers the unregistered **Covey** name and the Covey logos and app
icons (the “Marks”). They are used by Kashikuroni to identify the official
Covey project and its releases.

## Scope

The Apache License 2.0 grants copyright and patent permissions for the source
code. It does not grant permission to use the Marks. This policy does not
restrict rights granted by that license or by applicable law.

## Permitted use

You may use the Marks to make accurate, non-misleading statements about Covey,
for example to identify this repository as the original source or to state
that your project is a fork of Covey. Do not imply endorsement, sponsorship,
or an official relationship where none exists.

You may distribute an unmodified build of Covey under its original name and
with its original Marks, provided you preserve the copyright and attribution
notices required by the license.

## Forks and modified versions

If you distribute a modified version, choose a name and visual identity that
are clearly distinct from Covey. You may describe it factually as “a fork of
Covey”, but must not present it as an official Covey release.

Do not use the Covey name or its logos as the primary name, icon, domain name,
or social-media handle of a modified product in a way that is likely to cause
confusion about its origin, status, or support.

## Questions

If a proposed use is unclear, open an issue in the official repository before
using the Marks.
